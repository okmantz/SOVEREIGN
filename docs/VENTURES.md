# Ventures

A venture is a small experiment with a thesis, a budget and a loss limit. The Director opens them; the ledger judges them.

## Order of operations

1. **Hypothesis**: who pays, for what, reached how. The Critic attacks it before any money moves.
2. **Cheap test**: a landing page plus ~100–200 outreach touches or ~$50–100 of ads.
3. **Threshold**: set before the test. Cleared → scale budget. Not cleared → kill.
4. **Kill rule**: verified loss reaches the limit → stopped automatically.

## Starting bets, best fit first

1. Outreach-led service businesses (lead generation, appointment setting for local businesses): fastest to first revenue, agents do the actual work.
2. Niche micro-SaaS or tools sold by outreach plus paid traffic.
3. Digital products (templates, datasets, reports) sold through paid traffic.

Deliberately not first: content/SEO-only plays (slow) and dropshipping (margins vanish after ads and support).

## Where humans stay in the loop

Payment and identity verification, ad-account creation, legal review of offers, first outreach batch, and any budget increase. The station is designed so these are one-tap approvals, not chores.
