# Recipes

A recipe is a reusable business playbook. **`company-*.json`** are live: pick one in the Worlds panel ("Launch a company from a recipe") or `POST /api/launch/recipe`, and Sovereign creates a world of the right kind, sets the goal, creates the venture with the recipe's kill rules and KPIs, and drafts a plan for your approval. The team is the CEO, CFO and the specialists that kind of business needs; every role has a job and a playbook, and everything runs free by default.

`outreach-pilot.json` is the older draft format for a single experiment.
